Welcome to the Moissan Club newsletter. This is an **example issue** showing how a communication will look on the website. Replace this text with your first announcement when you are ready.

## What belongs here

This archive is a place to share the work and conversations behind the club:

- **Project notes:** what we tried, what we learned, and what comes next.
- **Community announcements:** opportunities to meet, contribute, or share an idea.
- **Practical discoveries:** tools, techniques, and resources useful to other builders.

## Start with the projects

If you are new to the club, explore our [projects](/projects/). You will find development helpers, browser-based video streaming, and documentation for working with the Unitree G1.

> Carbon sparks silicon. Shared ideas turn into things we can build.

## Keep in touch

Future communications will appear in the [newsletter archive](/newsletter/). You can also follow the [RSS feed](/newsletter/index.xml), continue reading the club's Google Group emails, or [contact us](mailto:info@moissan.org).
