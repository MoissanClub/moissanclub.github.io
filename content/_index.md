## Ideas grow when we build together.

Moissan Club is a community of robotics builders in Silicon Valley. We share practical knowledge, work across disciplines, and turn technical discussions into hands-on exploration.

Our projects and development notes are open invitations to look closer, ask questions, and build on what comes next.
